# Korea's Baby Comeback?
pillar: economy_money | sensitivity: go (review topic, approved with a hopeful framing)
hook_text: Korea's baby comeback? 👶

## EN
For years, Korea had the lowest birth rate on the planet. But lately? Something's changing.
In 2024, births in Korea went **up**, for the first time in almost a decade. Marriages jumped too.
So what's going on? Korea's been going all in. Parents can get monthly cash support for newborns, parental leave keeps getting better, and some companies are straight-up paying employees to have kids.
One company literally gave workers about **$70,000 per baby**. Yeah. Per baby.
It's still early, but for the first time in a long time, the trend is pointing up.
Would a bonus like that change *your* mind? 👀

2024년, 한국 출생아 수가 **늘었어**. 거의 10년 만에 처음으로. 결혼도 확 늘었고.
무슨 일이냐고? 한국이 올인하고 있거든. 아기가 태어나면 매달 현금 지원을 받을 수 있고, 육아휴직도 계속 좋아지고, 어떤 회사들은 아예 직원한테 아이 낳으라고 돈을 줘.
