# Korean Convenience Stores Are Different
pillar: society | sensitivity: go
hook_text: Korean convenience stores hit different

## EN
If you think convenience stores are boring… Korea would like a word.
First, they're **everywhere**. Like, you can often see another one from the door of the one you're in.
Inside? Fresh lunch boxes, hot ramen stations with free hot water, cheap snacks, and "1+1" deals everywhere. Buy one, get one free.
Some even have tables inside *and* outside, so it's basically a mini restaurant.
You can pay bills, send packages, and charge your transit card there too.
It's not just a store. It's a lifestyle.
