⚠️ 레딧은 올리기 전 각 커뮤니티 규칙(자기 홍보, 영상 링크 허용 여부)을 꼭 확인.

## 2. 레딧

### r/Busan 또는 r/korea (상어 꼭지)
- 제목: `Busan's North Port shark ("Bukang-i") drew 568,800 visitors in 10 days — about 50x a normal holiday`
- 본문:
```
I've been following the shark that swam into the canal at Busan's North Port and wouldn't leave.
A few numbers that surprised me:
- 568,800 visitors in ten days, 140,000 on a single Chuseok day
- The park normally gets about 2,000 people on a holiday
- It got a nickname within days: Bukang-i (Bukhang, "North Port" + the cute "-i" ending)

Anyone here actually go see it? Curious what it was like in person.
```
- 첫 댓글(링크): `I made a short animated news segment about it if anyone's interested: <롱폼 링크, 0:21 타임스탬프>`
- 참고: 숫자 출처는 부산시설공단(연합뉴스 보도). 댓글에서 물어보면 출처 링크로 답하기

### r/kpop (강남 꼭지, K-pop 꼭지가 있는 주에만)
- 제목: `Gangnam is launching a street festival it wants to be "the world's K-festival" — Psy is closing it`
- 본문은 행사 날짜·출연진 사실 위주로 짧게, 영상 링크는 댓글에. (올리는 날 행사 날짜가 지났으면 쓰지 않음)
